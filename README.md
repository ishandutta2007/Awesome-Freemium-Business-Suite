## Top Freemium Business Suite Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Free-Tier CRM, Project Management & Team Collaboration*  

**Last updated: March 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Freemium Business Suites**. These tools give teams a free starting point for managing customers, projects, and collaboration—with clear upgrade paths as organizations grow.



**Examples** include Salesforce Free Suite, Zoho One Free, Google Workspace Free Tier, HubSpot Free CRM, Bitrix24 Free, Notion Free Team, Airtable Free, Slack Free, ClickUp Free, and Asana Free (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with every major active project for self-hosting business suites with no per-seat fees and full data ownership—ideal for startups, small businesses, and developers seeking transparent, vendor-independent tools.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[Salesforce Free Suite](https://www.salesforce.com/)**  

  Free CRM tier with contact and opportunity management, lead capture, and basic reporting. Includes Salesforce Mobile app and AppExchange access. Ideal for small teams testing CRM workflows.



- **[Zoho One Free](https://www.zoho.com/)**  

  Free tier across Zoho's suite including CRM, Projects, Mail, and Docs. Generous free limits on users and records make it viable for startups and small teams.



- **[Google Workspace Free Tier](https://workspace.google.com/)**  

  Free personal Google account includes Docs, Sheets, Slides, Drive, and Meet with limited storage. Google Workspace Business starts paid, but personal accounts remain free.



- **[HubSpot Free CRM](https://www.hubspot.com/)**  

  Free forever CRM with contact management, deal pipelines, email tracking, and meeting scheduling. Includes marketing, sales, and service tools with upgrade paths.



- **[Bitrix24 Free](https://www.bitrix24.com/)**  

  Free plan supports unlimited users with CRM, tasks, projects, and 5 GB storage. Includes collaboration tools and basic automation.



- **[Notion Free Team](https://www.notion.so/)**  

  Free plan for unlimited users with collaborative docs, wikis, and basic databases. Limited block storage and file uploads.



- **[Airtable Free](https://www.airtable.com/)**  

  Free plan with unlimited bases, 1,000 records per base, and basic views. Popular for lightweight project tracking and databases.



- **[Slack Free](https://slack.com/)**  

  Free plan with 90-day message history, 10 app integrations, and 1:1 huddles. Best for small teams and evaluation.



- **[ClickUp Free](https://clickup.com/)**  

  Free Forever plan with unlimited users and tasks, 100 MB storage, and core project management views. Includes docs and whiteboards.



- **[Asana Free](https://asana.com/)**  

  Free plan for up to 10 teammates with unlimited tasks, projects, and basic views. Includes timeline and calendar views.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[Twenty](https://github.com/twentyhq/twenty)**  

  Modern open-source CRM (AGPL-3.0) built as a Salesforce alternative. Custom data models, Kanban pipelines, GraphQL/REST APIs, workflow automation, email/calendar sync, and role-based permissions. 45k+ GitHub stars. Self-hosted via Docker or Railway with no per-seat fees .



- **[SuiteCRM](https://github.com/SuiteCRM/SuiteCRM)**  

  Award-winning enterprise-class open-source CRM (AGPL-3.0). Successor to SugarCRM Community Edition with workflow automation, reporting, and plugin ecosystem. Large user base but dated UI and codebase .



- **[EspoCRM](https://github.com/espocrm/espocrm)**  

  Lightweight open-source CRM (AGPL-3.0) with intuitive interface, powerful in-app administration, roles manager, audit log, email sync, and fine-grained access control. Fast and performance-focused .



- **[Krayin](https://github.com/krayin/laravel-crm)**  

  Laravel-based open-source CRM (MIT) for SMEs with lead and pipeline management, Kanban boards, contact management, quotations, email integration, workflow automation, and REST API .



- **[Frappe CRM](https://github.com/frappe/crm)**  

  Modern, open-source CRM (AGPL-3.0) built on Frappe Framework. Features deal pipelines, contact management, email integration, and workflow automation. Part of the ERPNext ecosystem .



- **[Dolibarr](https://github.com/Dolibarr/dolibarr)**  

  Modular open-source ERP and CRM (GPL-3.0) for SMEs. Features contacts, leads, opportunities, quotations, invoices, and project management. Highly modular with extensive community modules .



- **[Odoo](https://github.com/odoo/odoo)**  

  Open-source business suite (LGPL-3.0) with CRM, Sales, Inventory, Accounting, and more. Features sales pipeline, lead scoring, quotations, and deep integration across modules. Large ecosystem with free Community edition .



- **[OpenProject](https://github.com/opf/openproject)**  

  Open-source project management (GPL-3.0) with classic and agile workflows, Gantt charts, agile boards, budget management, and stakeholder reporting. Self-hosted free with unlimited users. Cloud from €7.25/user/month .



- **[Taiga](https://github.com/taigaio/taiga)**  

  Open-source project management (AGPL-3.0) for Scrum and Kanban teams with beautiful UI. Features epics, swimlanes, wiki, and multi-project support. Self-hosted free .



- **[Leantime](https://github.com/Leantime/leantime)**  

  Open-source project management (AGPL-3.0) combining design thinking, lean, and Agile practices. Features kanban, Gantt, goal tracking, document manager, and AI capabilities. Free self-hosted .



- **[Plane](https://github.com/makeplane/plane)**  

  Modern open-source project management (Apache-2.0) positioned as a Jira alternative. Features issues, cycles, modules, multiple views, and GitHub sync. Rapidly growing community .



- **[Huly](https://github.com/hcengineering/platform)**  

  All-in-one open-source platform (EPL-2.0) combining project management, CRM, chat, HR, and wiki as an alternative to Linear, Jira, Slack, and Notion .



- **[Focalboard](https://github.com/mattermost/focalboard)**  

  Open-source self-hosted alternative to Trello, Notion, and Asana (MIT). Kanban boards, tables, and cards with Mattermost integration. 25k+ GitHub stars .



- **[Planka](https://github.com/plankanban/planka)**  

  Realtime kanban board for workgroups built with React and Redux (AGPL-3.0). Self-hosted Trello alternative .



- **[NocoDB](https://github.com/nocodb/nocodb)**  

  Open-source Airtable alternative (AGPL-3.0) that turns any database into a smart spreadsheet. Grid, gallery, kanban, form views, and auto-generated REST/GraphQL APIs. Scales to millions of rows .



- **[Baserow](https://github.com/baserow/baserow)**  

  Open-source no-code database (MIT) with polished Airtable-like interface. Plugin marketplace, real-time collaboration, form builder, and REST API. Self-hosted free with unlimited rows and users .



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **[Vikunja](https://github.com/go-vikunja/vikunja)** — Self-hosted todo and project management (AGPL-3.0) with list, kanban, Gantt, and CalDAV support .

- **[Wekan](https://github.com/wekan/wekan)** — Open-source kanban board (MIT) with full data ownership, Trello alternative .

- **[Kanboard](https://github.com/kanboard/kanboard)** — Fast, simple open-source task manager (MIT) for minimalist teams .

- **[Redmine](https://github.com/redmine/redmine)** — Flexible open-source issue tracking and project management (GPL-2.0) with plugin ecosystem .

- **[Zeropipeline MCP](https://pypi.org/project/zeropipeline-mcp/)** — Free, open-source AI-native CRM with MCP server, 30 AI agent tools, unlimited free tier, and self-hosting .



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Combine **Twenty** or **Krayin** as the CRM backend with **OpenProject** or **Plane** for project management, **NocoDB** or **Baserow** for no-code databases, and **PostgreSQL** for persistence—creating a fully self-hosted, enterprise-grade freemium business suite with no per-seat fees .
